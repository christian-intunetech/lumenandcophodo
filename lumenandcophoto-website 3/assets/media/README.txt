LUMEN & CO. WEBSITE MEDIA
=========================
Drop your files in this folder with these exact names. The site picks them up
automatically. Anything missing is simply skipped, so the site never shows a
broken image.

HERO BACKGROUND VIDEO (plays silently behind the top of the page)
  hero.mp4          10–20 second loop, landscape (16:9), 1920x1080 or 1280x720,
                    MP4 (H.264), no sound needed, ideally under 8 MB.
  hero-poster.jpg   A still frame from the video (shows while it loads).

"RECENT EVENTS" PHOTO GALLERY (section appears once at least one photo exists)
  gallery-1.jpg ... gallery-6.jpg
                    Portrait (4:5) works best, about 1200x1500, JPG under 500 KB each.

Tip: send the raw clips/photos to Claude and it can trim, compress and rename them.
