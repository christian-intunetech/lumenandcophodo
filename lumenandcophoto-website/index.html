FAQ

Good questions.

How much space does the booth need?

The booth itself has a small footprint. We recommend an area of about 10 by 10 feet so guests have room to gather, pose and use props.

What do you need from the venue?

A standard power outlet near the booth location and a level indoor surface. For outdoor events, the booth needs a covered, shaded spot.

When do you arrive to set up?

We arrive about an hour before your booth time starts, so everything is ready before guests arrive. Setup time is not counted against your package hours.

How do guests get their photos?

Right at the booth, by text, email or QR code. After the event, you receive a link to an online gallery with every photo.

Can the photos be branded for our event?

Yes. Every package includes a custom photo overlay with your names, date or event theme. The Signature package adds full custom branding with your logo.

How do I reserve my date?
